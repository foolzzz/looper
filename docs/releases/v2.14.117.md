# Looper v2.14.117

This is the patch release v2.14.117, a hotfix line in which each merged change ships at once. It ships the one change merged since v2.14.116: a review panel built on dependency conditions ([#1183](https://github.com/foolzzz/looper/pull/1183)).

## Release Decision

**Who should upgrade:** Operators who use workflow presets that define a `review_panel`. Installations and tasks without such a workflow see no change in behavior, apart from the one-time database migration below.

**What this release solves:** [#1183](https://github.com/foolzzz/looper/pull/1183). A workflow can now define a review panel: each step that needs review gets one parallel review step per panel member, and the step is accepted when an acceptance condition over those members holds, evaluated by the same dependency-condition evaluator. Findings carry a severity and are merged per issue, suggestions do not block, late verdicts are collected, and the developer and the reviewers must run on different runtimes.

**Breaking changes:** No. The database gains two columns, a step kind and three journal kinds, applied on first start; tasks and plans without a workflow panel behave as before. One data change applies to stored presets, see Upgrading.

**How to verify:** Check the installed version.

```bash
looper --version   # looper 2.14.117
```

**Contributors:** Maintainer @foolzzz authored [#1183](https://github.com/foolzzz/looper/pull/1183); no community contribution was included in this release.

## Upgrading

The bridge boundary is unchanged: an installation older than v2.13.165 must upgrade to v2.13.165 first (upgrade to v2.13.165 first) and be started once; see the Upgrading section of the v2.14.0 release for the steps and rollback. An installation on v2.14.0 to v2.14.116 upgrades directly. This release has a database change, applied automatically on the first start and recorded once as the `review_panel` migration: `task_steps` gains `accept_when` and `reviews` columns and its kind check accepts `review`; the `task_journal` kind check accepts `review_round`, `late_review` and `review_adjudicated`. The migration also sets `distinct_reviewer_runtime` to true in every stored workflow preset that has a `review_panel`, because a panel now requires it; the snapshots of existing tasks stay as they are. Because the schema and presets change, take the usual database backup before upgrading; an older binary should not be started on the upgraded database without restoring it.

## Behavior changes

- **Panel expansion.** With a workflow `review_panel` (`members`, each a review role or `builtin`, and an `accept_when` condition over them), each step that needs review gets one review step per member, depending on it and ready when it completes. The step's `accept_when` is the panel condition over those seats. A panel needs at least one required reviewer and may omit the built-in Reviewer. A verification-role step is never reviewed. Steps that need no review get no seats, and pending seats of a step that stopped needing review are skipped.
- **Acceptance.** The panel condition is evaluated by the dependency-condition evaluator over each seat's verdict (`passed`), so quorums such as `k_of` work and the step is decided as soon as the condition is satisfied or cannot be satisfied. Seats outside the condition are advisory.
- **Severity findings.** Panel reviewers report findings as `{severity, where, text}` with severity `blocking` or `suggestion`; `passed` is true only when no finding is blocking, and an inconsistent verdict is turned into a fail. Findings from all members are merged by location in the report to the Planner and stored on the step (`verify_passed`, `verify_findings`). Suggestions do not block; the Planner must take or decline each one and record the reason.
- **Late verdicts.** A seat that finishes after its step was accepted is journaled (`late_review`). A late blocking finding reopens the step so the Planner decides again.
- **Review rounds.** Each settled round is journaled (`review_round`); a retry resets the seats and discards stale ones.
- **Adjudication.** When the workflow sets `adjudication`, the Planner may, on a review decision, downgrade an existing blocking finding to a suggestion with a reason of 1 to 500 characters (`adjudications`: member, finding index, level `suggestion`, reason). The downgrade is journaled (`review_adjudicated`), the step may be accepted only if the acceptance condition then holds, and the delivered summary lists the adjudications.
- **Different runtimes.** A panel requires `distinct_reviewer_runtime`. A member that resolves the same runtime as the developer is refused with `same_runtime` and counts as a failed review.
- **Stable head.** Each seat reviews the same frozen commit in its own checkout (snapshot or frozen step workspace). A seat that finds the branch head moved fails with `head_moved`; if the checkout cannot be made the seat fails with `snapshot_unavailable`. When a step is merged, its frozen review result is merged instead of committing leftovers.
- **Fixed panel.** In a workflow with a panel, the Planner may not write `review_roles`, review steps or `accept_when`; the workflow fixes the panel. Panel seats do not hold the task's completion summary; a last work step with remaining seats must carry a `final` delivery summary.
- **Views and dashboard.** The task view gains `accept_when`, `accept_state`, `accept_when_text`, `reviews` and step kind `review`. The pipeline view draws the acceptance condition as a junction between the seats and later work.
- **Tests and evals.** The prompt-regression set gains `panel_merge` and `panel_adjudicate`.
- **Model-visible changes:** Yes, for tasks whose workflow has a `review_panel`. The Planner's system prompt says that the workflow fixes the review panel and not to write `review_roles`, review steps or `accept_when`, describes the work role rule, and, with `adjudication`, the adjudications format and limits. Decision prompts carry the panel report (per member: required or advisory, PASS, FAIL or pending, blocking count, then findings by location), the instruction to take or decline suggestions, and the `final` requirement for a last work step. Reviewers receive a verdict contract with severity findings (`severity`, `where`, `text`) and `passed` only without blocking findings. The task summary gains a "Review adjudications" section and the Planner's journal view the new review kinds. Tasks and workflows without a panel keep their prior prompts and contract.
- **Unchanged:** plans and tasks without a workflow panel, ordinary review steps and their contract, the dependency-condition syntax.

### 升级决策

**谁需要升级：**使用定义了 `review_panel` 的工作流预设的运维者。没有该工作流的安装和任务行为无变化（仅有下述一次性数据库迁移）。

**是否有破坏性变更：**否。数据库新增两列、一种步骤类型和三种日志类型，首次启动时自动应用；没有工作流评审小组的任务和计划行为不变。已存储的预设有一处数据变更，见升级一节。

**如何验证：**核对版本为 2.14.117。

### 升级

桥接边界不变：早于 v2.13.165 的安装必须先升级到 v2.13.165 并启动一次（upgrade to v2.13.165 first 即先升级到 v2.13.165），步骤与回退见 v2.14.0 发布说明的升级一节。v2.14.0 至 v2.14.116 上的安装可直接升级。本版本有数据库变更，首次启动时自动应用并以 `review_panel` 迁移记录一次：`task_steps` 新增 `accept_when` 和 `reviews` 列，其类型检查接受 `review`；`task_journal` 的类型检查接受 `review_round`、`late_review` 和 `review_adjudicated`。迁移还会把所有带 `review_panel` 的已存储工作流预设的 `distinct_reviewer_runtime` 设为 true（小组现在要求它）；已有任务的快照不变。由于结构和预设有变，升级前请照常备份数据库；未恢复备份时不要在已升级的数据库上启动旧版本。

### 行为变化与入口

- **小组展开。**工作流的 `review_panel`（`members`，每个成员是评审角色或 `builtin`，以及作用于它们的 `accept_when` 条件）使每个需要评审的步骤按成员各得到一个评审步骤，依赖该步骤并在其完成时就绪。该步骤的 `accept_when` 即作用于这些评审席位的小组条件。小组至少需要一个必选评审者，可以不含内置 Reviewer。验证角色的步骤不被评审。无需评审的步骤没有席位，不再需要评审的步骤其待处理席位会被跳过。
- **验收。**小组条件由依赖条件求值器按各席位的结论（`passed`）计算，因此支持 `k_of` 等多数条件；条件满足或不可能满足时立即决定。条件之外的席位仅作参考。
- **严重级别发现。**小组评审者以 `{severity, where, text}` 报告发现，严重级别为 `blocking` 或 `suggestion`；仅当没有阻塞性发现时 `passed` 才为 true，自相矛盾的结论会被改判为不通过。各成员的发现按位置合并后交给 Planner 并存入步骤（`verify_passed`、`verify_findings`）。建议不阻塞；Planner 须逐条采纳或拒绝并记录原因。
- **迟到的结论。**步骤被接受后才结束的席位记入日志（`late_review`）。迟到的阻塞性发现会重新打开该步骤，由 Planner 再次决定。
- **评审轮次。**每个结算的轮次记入日志（`review_round`）；重试会重置席位并丢弃过期席位。
- **裁定。**工作流设置 `adjudication` 时，Planner 可在评审决策中把已有的阻塞性发现降级为建议，并给出 1 到 500 字符的理由（`adjudications`：成员、发现序号、级别 `suggestion`、理由）。降级记入日志（`review_adjudicated`），仅当降级后验收条件成立时才可接受，交付摘要会列出裁定。
- **不同运行时。**小组要求 `distinct_reviewer_runtime`。解析出与开发者相同运行时的成员会以 `same_runtime` 被拒绝，计为评审失败。
- **稳定的 HEAD。**每个席位在自己的检出中评审同一个冻结的提交（快照或冻结的步骤工作区）。发现分支 HEAD 已移动的席位以 `head_moved` 失败；无法建立检出时以 `snapshot_unavailable` 失败。步骤合并时合并其冻结的评审结果，而不是提交遗留改动。
- **固定的小组。**有小组的工作流中，Planner 不得写 `review_roles`、评审步骤或 `accept_when`，小组由工作流固定。评审席位不阻挡任务的完成摘要；最后一个工作步骤若还有席位未完成，必须带 `final` 交付摘要。
- **视图与仪表盘。**任务视图新增 `accept_when`、`accept_state`、`accept_when_text`、`reviews` 和步骤类型 `review`。流水线视图把验收条件画成评审席位与后续工作之间的汇合点。
- **测试与评测。**提示词回归集新增 `panel_merge` 和 `panel_adjudicate`。
- **面向模型的变化：**对工作流有 `review_panel` 的任务有。Planner 系统提示词说明工作流固定评审小组、不要写 `review_roles`、评审步骤或 `accept_when`，说明工作角色规则，并在 `adjudication` 时说明裁定的格式与限制。决策提示词带有小组报告（每个成员：必选或参考、PASS、FAIL 或 pending、阻塞数，随后按位置列出发现）、采纳或拒绝建议的指示，以及最后一个工作步骤的 `final` 要求。评审者收到带严重级别发现（`severity`、`where`、`text`）的结论契约，且仅在没有阻塞性发现时 `passed` 为 true。任务摘要新增“Review adjudications”一节，Planner 的日志视图新增评审类型。没有小组的任务和工作流提示词与契约不变。
- **不变：**没有工作流评审小组的计划和任务、普通评审步骤及其契约、依赖条件的语法。
